LAYER 6F — VERIFIABLE TRAINING PIPELINE

The Final Frontier: Prove Not Just Inference, But Training Itself

Layer 6F represents the frontier of verifiable computation: proving that the training process itself was executed correctly, not just the final inference. By leveraging advanced folding schemes and recursive proof composition, we can compress multi-epoch training runs into succinct, efficiently verifiable proofs—enabling trustless model training at scale.

📚

Training Data Compliance

Cryptographic commitments to training datasets ensure data provenance and compliance with privacy regulations without revealing sensitive information.

🌳

Protocol Verification

ZK proofs verify that the training protocol—gradient descent, regularization, and hyperparameter updates—was executed exactly as specified.

💾

Weight Integrity

Final model weights are bound to the training proof, ensuring that any deployed model can be traced back to its verified training run.

Powered by Advanced Folding

Our implementation leverages Nova-style folding schemes to achieve sub-linear verification time for training proofs. By recursively folding multiple training step proofs into a single succinct proof, we dramatically reduce the computational burden on verifiers while maintaining cryptographic soundness.

  • ✓ Sub-linear verification time
  • ✓ O(1) recursion overhead
  • ✓ Distributed prover networking
