# Economic Proof: AgentGuardian Nexus

## Margin Analysis: Traditional vs. Arc
Traditional Ethereum gas costs fluctuate based on network congestion, often ranging from $2.00 to $50.00 for complex multi-step interactions.

### The Agentic Economy Challenge
Autonomous agents executing high-frequency micro-transactions (e.g., 50+ per hour for API calls or compute balancing) on Ethereum would consume more in gas than the value of the transaction itself.

## Why AgentGuardian Nexus Wins
1.  **Deterministic Economics:** By using Arc’s native USDC settlement, costs are predictable. 
2.  **Collateral Efficiency:** Reputation-as-Collateral (rAGNT) ensures that high-trust agents pay lower insurance premiums, effectively subsidizing their own transaction costs through earned reputation.
3.  **Risk-Adjusted Margin:** The integrated `InsurancePool` allows risk-averse enterprises to cover their agents, converting variable "black-box" risk into a predictable, hedgeable insurance expense.

## Conclusion
AgentGuardian provides a stable, audit-ready framework that enables machine-to-machine commerce to scale where traditional infrastructure fails.
